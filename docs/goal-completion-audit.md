# Optimization goal completion audit — 2026-09-27

The nine implementation priorities are delivered as a tested local optimization
platform. This audit records their acceptance evidence and production boundaries;
it does not claim frontier-lab adoption, general NP-hard problem solutions, live
model superiority, or production-scale validation.

| Requirement | Current authoritative evidence | Explicit scope boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
